Use the numbered stages to reveal the calculation. Sensor returns and simulated spikes are computed at each movement step, even if their plots are initially hidden.

Scope and assumptions. This is a synthetic, reproducible border-cell-like teaching model, not a biological recording, not a learned border cell, and not a robot policy derived from spikes. Pose is exact; the boundary mode is chosen externally; the single-wall option identifies a specified wall/arc; LiDAR ray-casting is noise-free. In Stage 5, white bins are unvisited rather than silent. The stochastic trajectory has a visible random seed, follows synthetic boundary-aware safety rules, and uses a 0.15 m point-robot guard (no simulated chassis radius). The map divides actual generated spike counts by accumulated dwell time (0.16 s per step), and is not a fitted Gaussian image.
