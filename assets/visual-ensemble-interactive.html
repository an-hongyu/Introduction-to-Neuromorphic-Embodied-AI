Interpretation: This is a synthetic geometry-and-RF feasibility experiment, not a trained Spike Flow Network. The class scores are uncalibrated heuristic activations, not probabilities. Strong tilt, clipping, occlusion, and noise are not guaranteed. A non-equilateral triangle remains a triangle; image-only inference cannot always determine whether its 3D source was equilateral.
