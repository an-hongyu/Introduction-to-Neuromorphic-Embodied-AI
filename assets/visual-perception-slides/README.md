# Visual Perception slide illustrations

These figures were extracted from Hongyu An's two supplied Visual Perception lecture files and inserted into Chapter 6 at the author's request. They are original slide assets, not AI-generated redraws. The PowerPoint decks themselves are not redistributed here.

The manifest records the source file, 1-based slide position, source-image SHA-256 when applicable, output SHA-256, dimensions, and any crop or panel arrangement. Native image labels, figure captions, logos, and visible credits have been retained. The two ray/aperture diagrams were rendered from the original slide objects and cropped to remove the surrounding slide title, footer, and repeated body text.

Third-party material retains its original rights. Inclusion in these supplied teaching slides does not establish a new open license; the repository's text/code license does not relicense these illustrations. Where a slide gives no precise original-source attribution, the manifest says so rather than inventing a citation or permission status. All displayed captions cite the corresponding course deck and slide position.
