Grid → Place synaptic weights (18 × 12)

Rows are Place Cells, columns are Grid Cells. Blue/negative = inhibitory contribution; positive = excitatory contribution. Ridge: signed fitted weights. Coincidence: binary top-six connections. Pattern: all 12 Grid inputs with inhibitory mismatch. No online learning.

Each sector uses its nearest valid return. 0° is forward, angles increase to the left. Invalid, stale, or missing measurements supply zero current and retain an explicit status. Measured rates use a 0.5 s window; the first window is a warmup.

Coverage counts safe robot-center grid locations within 0.20 m of observed centers. It is not floor area or footprint-swept area. Navigation uses the known map; neither red cue, reward, nor neural firing chooses the robot’s target. This is engineered proximity coding, not a demonstrated biological border-cell law.

Place/grid tuning follows the explicit equations in the Liu–Jin–Song–An JETCAS author revision dated 29 June 2026 (Eqs. 8 and 13). The 12 grid units, 18 fixed place centers, grid spacings and 40 Hz readout peak are T-maze engineering choices. The full learned grid-to-place circuit, DEN protocol and multisensory gating are not reproduced. Reference fields are predictions; observed maps use actual counted spikes divided by occupancy.
